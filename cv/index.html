Summary

Physicist (PhD, Condensed Matter Physics, University of Oslo, 2023) working across research, teaching, and science communication. Currently Senior Lecturer at the University of Oslo's Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences, researching the use of AI in education, with a particular focus on science and mathematics teaching — including a forthcoming book, Lære med KI. Formerly a physics and mathematics teacher in Oslo through Teach First Norway. Co-founder and host of the science podcast Under Kappa, recurring panelist on NRK's Abels tårn, and recipient of the Norwegian Physical Society's 2025 outreach prize.
